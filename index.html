Voices Behind the Mission

Hear from the founders who started it all.

This program has really changed the way I think and hopefully others too. In a world full of brainrot, and social media, this reading program will help young readers advance, understand and appreciate literature. My friends and I want to help kids and change their lives.

- Sahana, Founder

Turn the page has been a life-saver. Getting to put in effort into something I know will help many people changed how I see things. Sharing the love I have for reading feels better than really anything.

I love to read because I was introduced to it so young. I know so many people around my age who don't like reading, and i want to let generations to come learn to love it like i do.
